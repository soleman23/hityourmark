Section 08

Travel Insurance

We strongly recommend that all travelers purchase comprehensive travel insurance for every trip. This is one of the most important pieces of advice we can give you, and we say it with conviction: the question is never whether something can go wrong — it's whether you're protected when it does.

What to Look For
  • Trip cancellation and interruption coverage (for illness, injury, family emergency, and "cancel for any reason" if available)
  • Medical expense coverage, including emergency evacuation and repatriation
  • Baggage loss, delay, and theft coverage
  • Travel delay coverage
  • Coverage adequate for the total non-refundable trip cost
Important Notes on Coverage

Pre-existing condition waivers typically require purchasing coverage within a short window of your initial deposit — often 10 to 21 days. We recommend purchasing insurance promptly after your first deposit. Policy terms and coverage details vary significantly between providers. We are not licensed insurance agents and cannot advise on the specific suitability of any policy for your circumstances. Read your policy documents carefully and contact your insurer directly with coverage questions.

Declining travel insurance is your right. If you choose to travel without it, you accept full financial responsibility for any losses not covered by Supplier refund policies.
