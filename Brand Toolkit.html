Identity 01

Logo Usage

The shield and compass rose is the brand mark. Use it with confidence and precision — it has presence. Don't crowd it, invert it, or decorate around it.

On Navy — Primary use
On Cream
On White
Do
  • Use on deep navy — this is the home environment
  • Maintain clear space equal to the logo's width on all sides
  • Use full mark on dark or light neutral backgrounds only
  • Scale proportionally, never distort
  • Use the gold drop shadow on dark backgrounds at small sizes
Don't
  • Place on busy photography without a dark overlay
  • Add outlines, drop shadows, or effects not in this guide
  • Recreate or redraw any element of the shield or compass
  • Use on colored backgrounds — only navy, white, or cream
  • Rotate, skew, or stretch in any direction
Identity 02

Voice & Tone

Four pillars govern every word we publish. The test: would this appear in Condé Nast Traveller or Departures? If not, rewrite it.

Warm
A friend who's been everywhere. Genuine, conversational, never scripted. You love this work and it shows in specific, human detail.
Confident
World traveler with a lifetime of experience. You state things plainly. No pitching, no overselling, no exclamation points.
Editorial
Long, considered sentences. Specific sensory detail over generic adjectives. Name the reef, the wine, the view — not the "stunning ocean vistas."
Never Salesy
No "Book now." No "Don't miss out." No urgency theater. The only CTA is "Plan My Trip" — an invitation, not a transaction.
Before & After
✕ Never write this
"Stunning luxury villa with amazing ocean views! Book now before it's gone — this incredible deal won't last. Limited availability for your dream vacation in paradise!"
✓ Write this instead
"The villa sits on a coral cliff above Petit Cul-de-Sac, with a private path down to the reef. Your chef arrives at six, the wine is already chilled, and the only decision left is which direction to swim."
✕ Never write this
"We offer amazing travel deals for the whole family! Our incredible team is ready to book your trip of a lifetime at unbeatable rates. Contact us today!"
✓ Write this instead
"Booking engines sell inventory. We design journeys. A lifetime of traveling and a global network that gets our clients perks and access simply not available any other way."
Words & Phrases to Avoid
Amazing / Incredible / Stunning
Dream vacation
Trip of a lifetime
Paradise
Book now!

Limited time offer
Rates from…

We offer… / We provide…
The One CTA Rule
Plan My Trip
This is the only call-to-action. It's an invitation, not a transaction. It implies collaboration, expertise, and trust — not urgency or scarcity. Do not write "Book Now," "Contact Us," or "Get a Quote."
