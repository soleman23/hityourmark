Section 01

Who We Are

Hit Your Mark Travel ("HYM Travel," "we," "us," or "our") is a licensed independent travel advisory firm based in California. We provide personalized travel planning and booking services to individual and group clients. This Privacy Policy applies to all personal information collected through our website at hymtravel.com, via email and phone communications, and in connection with our travel planning services.

We are registered sellers of travel in California (License No. 2165910-50), Washington (License No. 605920581), and Florida (License No. ST46122). For questions about this policy, see Section 14.

Section 02

Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide you with travel planning services, process your bookings, and communicate with you effectively. The categories of personal information we may collect are:

Information You Give Us Directly
  • Identity information: Full legal name, date of birth, nationality, gender
  • Contact information: Email address, phone number, mailing address
  • Passport and travel document details: Passport number, expiration date, country of issue — required to make international bookings
  • Payment information: Credit card details processed through secure payment systems (we do not store full card numbers)
  • Travel preferences: Dietary requirements, accessibility needs, room preferences, loyalty program numbers, travel style and budget parameters
  • Trip inquiry details: Destination interests, travel dates, group composition, special occasions
  • Communications: Emails, messages, and notes exchanged during the planning process
Information Collected Automatically
  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent on site, links clicked, referring URLs
  • Device and browser information: IP address, browser type, operating system, screen resolution
  • Cookie data: See Section 06 for full details on cookies and tracking
Information from Third Parties
  • Referrals: If another client refers you, they may share your name and contact information with us
  • Supplier confirmations: Booking confirmations and updates from hotels, airlines, and other suppliers may contain information about your trip
Section 03

How We Collect Information

We collect information through the following channels:

  • Trip inquiry forms on our website, where you voluntarily submit contact and travel details
  • Direct email and phone communication during the planning and booking process
  • Payment processing when you authorize deposits or final payments
  • Website analytics tools that track site usage automatically (see Section 06)
  • Email communication tools that may track whether emails are opened or links are clicked
  • Referrals from existing clients with your knowledge

We do not purchase contact lists or acquire personal information from data brokers. Every contact we have was initiated by the individual or by someone who referred them to us.

Section 04

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information only for purposes directly related to providing our services and running our business. Specifically:

Purpose Information Used
Respond to trip inquiries and assess fit Name, contact info, travel preferences, inquiry details
Design and deliver personalized itineraries Travel preferences, budget, group composition, special needs
Make reservations with hotels, airlines, and other suppliers Full legal name, date of birth, passport details, payment info
Process payments and issue receipts Payment information, contact info
Communicate trip updates, confirmations, and reminders Contact info, booking details
Provide in-trip and post-trip support Contact info, trip itinerary details
Send occasional newsletters or destination inspiration (opt-in only) Name, email address
Improve our website and services Anonymized usage and analytics data
Comply with legal obligations Identity and booking records as required

We do not use your personal information to build advertising profiles, sell to third parties, or make automated decisions that affect you without human review.

Section 05

Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

With Travel Suppliers

To book your travel, we must share relevant personal information with hotels, airlines, cruise lines, tour operators, ground transportation providers, and other suppliers. This sharing is necessary to complete your reservation and is limited to what each supplier requires. Each supplier operates under its own privacy policy once your information is in their system.

With Service Providers

We may use third-party vendors to help operate our business — including payment processors, email platforms, scheduling tools, and website hosting services. These vendors are permitted to use your information only to provide services to us and are prohibited from using it for their own marketing purposes.

For Legal Compliance

We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or government authority, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of HYM Travel, our clients, or others.

Business Transfers

In the event that Hit Your Mark Travel is sold, merged, or its assets transferred, client information may be part of the transferred assets. We would notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

We will never sell your personal information. This is not a standard disclaimer — it is a firm commitment. Our business is built on client trust. Selling your data would be inconsistent with everything we stand for.

Section 06

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience and understand how our site is used. A cookie is a small text file stored on your device when you visit a website.

Types of Cookies We Use
  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand traffic patterns, popular pages, and how visitors interact with the site. We use this data in aggregate and anonymized form to improve our content.
  • Preference cookies: Remember settings you've chosen, such as language or display preferences.
  • Marketing cookies: If enabled, track visits across websites to allow relevant advertising. We use these sparingly and only in ways that respect user privacy.
Your Cookie Choices

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website. You may also opt out of certain analytics tracking by using browser privacy tools or opt-out mechanisms provided by analytics platforms we use.

Email Tracking

Our email communications may include tracking pixels that indicate whether an email was opened and whether links were clicked. This helps us send more relevant follow-ups and understand which content is useful to clients. You can disable image loading in your email client to prevent this tracking.

Section 07

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Our general retention practices are:

  • Active client records: Retained for the duration of our working relationship and for a period of seven (7) years afterward, to support potential future bookings, warranty claims, and legal requirements
  • Booking and payment records: Retained for a minimum of seven (7) years in compliance with California business and tax record requirements
  • Trip inquiry records (no booking made): Retained for up to two (2) years, then deleted or anonymized
  • Email newsletter subscribers: Retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion
  • Website analytics data: Retained in aggregate, anonymized form with no fixed expiration

When your information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it. You may request earlier deletion of your records — see Section 08.

Section 08

Your Rights & Choices

Regardless of where you live, we give you meaningful control over your personal information. You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements and active booking obligations
  • Portability: Request that we provide your information in a structured, commonly used format
  • Opt out of marketing: Unsubscribe from newsletters or promotional emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email, or by contacting us directly
  • Withdraw consent: Where we rely on your consent to process information, you may withdraw it at any time — though this will not affect processing that occurred before the withdrawal

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at mark@hymtravel.com. We will respond within 30 days and will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests.

Note: If you have an active booking, some information cannot be deleted until after your travel is complete and our legal retention periods have passed. We'll explain this clearly if it applies to your request.

Section 09

California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).

Categories of Personal Information Collected

In the past 12 months, we have collected the following categories of personal information: identifiers (name, email, address, phone, IP address); personal information per California Civil Code §1798.80 (name, payment card information, passport details); internet or other network activity (website usage data); travel preferences and trip history.

No Sale of Personal Information

We do not sell personal information as defined under the CCPA/CPRA, and we have not done so in the past 12 months. We do not share personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Your California Rights
  • Right to Know: Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected about you, the categories of sources, business purposes for collection, and categories of third parties we share with
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information we have collected, subject to exceptions
  • Right to Correct: Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Right to Opt Out of Sale/Sharing: We do not sell or share information for advertising — this right is satisfied by our business practices
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information: We use sensitive information (passport details, payment data) only as necessary to provide our services
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights
Submitting a California Rights Request

To submit a verifiable consumer request under the CCPA/CPRA, email us at mark@hymtravel.com with "California Privacy Request" in the subject line. We will verify your identity and respond within 45 days, with a possible 45-day extension where reasonably necessary.

Section 10

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it promptly.

When booking travel for family groups that include minors, we collect information about minor travelers (such as names and dates of birth) only as required to complete supplier bookings. This information is provided by the parent or guardian, who is responsible for authorizing its collection and use on behalf of the minor.

Section 11

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. These measures include:

  • Secure, encrypted connections (HTTPS) for data transmission on our website
  • Use of reputable, security-compliant payment processors — we do not store full payment card numbers
  • Access controls limiting who within our organization can access client information
  • Regular review of our security practices and third-party vendor security standards

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. While we work diligently to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

Please protect your own security by using strong, unique passwords for any accounts, not sharing login credentials, and contacting us immediately if you suspect any unauthorized activity.

Section 12

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites — including supplier booking portals, destination resources, and travel review platforms. This Privacy Policy applies only to Hit Your Mark Travel and our website. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of any third-party sites. When you follow a link to another website, we encourage you to review that site's privacy policy.

Our supplier partners — including hotels, airlines, and tour operators — operate under their own privacy policies. When we make a booking on your behalf, your information becomes subject to both our policy and the supplier's policy. We work only with reputable suppliers, but we cannot guarantee their privacy practices.

Section 13

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or the services we provide. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify active clients by email.

Your continued use of our services after any change to this policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms. We encourage you to review this page periodically. The current version is always available at hymtravel.com/privacy.
